DESIGN DECISION
PriorityQueue is suitable for emergency triage.
Lower severity number receives higher priority.
Arrival order is maintained for equal severity.
Adding a patient: O(log n)
Removing next patient: O(log n)
A plain FIFO queue cannot handle severity priority.

FAIRNESS NOTE
Minor patients may wait longer during continuous emergencies.
Priority can be increased based on waiting time.
