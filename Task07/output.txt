TASK 07 - DESIGN CHALLENGE
==========================

BOTTLENECK DIAGNOSIS
Original design sorts the list for every ranking request.
Repeated sorting wastes processing time.

REDESIGN OBJECTIVE
Sort once and reuse the sorted ranking.
Avoid unnecessary repeated sorting.
